[publication]
title = YoloV8-Powered Surveillance: Safeguarding Crops from Wildlife and Fire Hazards
authors = 
venue = IEEE
year = 24 Jun, 2024
summary = Agriculture is the backbone of India’s economy. However, the farmers are facing crop damage due to wild animals encounter and fire. The study has mainly intended to provide a solution for the prevention of wildife encounters into agricultural field and fire using Object Detection. This study mainly focuses on detection of fire and four distinct animals: Elephant, Wild Boar, Deer and Cow. The dataset contains 7000 images with annotated five categories of Object. We trained five YOLOv8 models (nano - YOLOv8n, small - YOLOv8s, medium - YOLOv8m, large - YOLOv81, extra-large — YOLOv8x) on our dataset. Raspberry Pi equipped with a Camera captures video of the crop field and applies Yolov8 (Object Detection Algorithm) to detect fire and wildife encounters and plays some loud noises to deter wild animals. The Farmer is notified with a photograph of Farm and class name of the animal detection made through a Telegram Bot. The trained medium YOLOv8m model has attained a mAP of 92.2%, proving its efficacy in identifying fire and wild animals. By integrating this device across farm field, real-time automated monitoring and alerts can be provided to farmers, minimizing crop losses.
